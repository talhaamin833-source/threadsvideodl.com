Threads Video Downloader: an open guide

Updated November 12, 2026 · 3 min read

Threads, the text-and-media app from Instagram's makers, lets people post short videos alongside their updates. The app has no "save video" button for other people's posts, which is why so many people search for a Threads video downloader. This open guide explains, in plain language, how saving a public Threads video works, what you actually receive, and how to pick a tool that respects both you and the creator.

How a Threads video link is built

Every public Threads post has a permanent address. When you tap Share → Copy link, you get something like https://www.threads.com/@username/post/ABC123xyz. The part after /post/ is the post's short code. A downloader reads that code, asks Threads for the public media attached to the post, and returns the original video file. Nothing is recorded from your screen, so the result is the same file Threads streams to everyone.

Two things follow from this. First, only public posts can be saved; a private account's media is never returned. Second, you get the creator's original upload, not a re-encoded copy, so quality is as good as Threads allows.

The three-step workflow

  1. Copy the post link from the Threads app or website.
  2. Paste it into a web downloader such as ThreadsVideoDL.
  3. Choose the file (video, photo or audio) and save it to your device.

Watermarks and why they don't appear

Unlike some short-video apps, Threads does not burn a logo into the video when it is served. A downloader that fetches the original file therefore gives you a clean copy. Tools that add their own branding are doing so by choice. If a clean file matters for your workflow, look for a Threads video downloader without watermark and check a sample before relying on it.

Choosing a tool: an open checklist

  • No login required. A downloader never needs your Threads or Instagram password. Treat any tool that asks for one as unsafe.
  • No forced app install. A browser-based tool works on iPhone, Android and PC without extra permissions.
  • Original quality. Compare the file size and resolution with what you see in the app.
  • Clear policies. A real privacy policy, a DMCA contact and an explanation of what is (and isn't) stored.
  • Few, honest ads. Fake "Download" buttons are a red flag.

If you save clips often from a desktop browser, a lightweight Threads downloader browser extension or bookmarklet removes the copy-paste step entirely.

Common questions

Does the creator get notified?

No. Saving a public file doesn't interact with the post, so there is no like, view or notification attached to it.

Can I save videos from private accounts?

No, and a trustworthy tool won't try. Private media is only available to approved followers inside the app.

Why does a link sometimes fail?

The post may have been deleted, the account may have gone private, or the link may be a profile link rather than a post link. Copy the link again from the post's share menu and retry.
